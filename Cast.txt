Sasha Baptiste and Ray Hackner are BOTH MEN — a two-man live act. Sasha is white (dark swept-back hair, jaw stubble); Ray is Black (short buzzed crop, clean-shaven). They headlined the sold-out finale show of their tour at Wembley Stadium, performing "Never Ask What Could've Been".

CONCEPT — Logline: on the last night of their tour at Wembley, two frontmen play a song about refusing to look back, and by the final chorus both men know it is the last time they will ever share a stage. Engine: dramatic irony curdling into grief — ninety thousand people are euphoric while the two men on stage are quietly coming apart; the audience reads it in their faces before the crowd does. The song title IS the twist: they sing "never ask what could've been" on the very night that becomes the thing everyone will ask about.
