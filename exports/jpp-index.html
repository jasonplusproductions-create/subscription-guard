What is Arrangements?

A Modern Dramedy

On one charged night at a queer bar, a couple tests the limits of their open "arrangement" while the people around them reveal the agreements they've made about love, sex, money, and safety. Nothing here is "natural"—every relationship is an arrangement someone agreed to, or didn't.

This isn't a show about George Michael. It's a contemporary story about us—scored with his timeless music. Performances at Free Range Brewery, NoDa — postponed, new dates to be announced.

"The two words that seem to most define George Michael—at least through his discography—are freedom and faith."

— Medium, 2016
